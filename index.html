HuntingBadGuys.

A Threat Infrastructure Intelligence platform that I designed and built end-to-end. It analyses and correlates malicious infrastructure at scale: domains, IPs, certificates, ASN data and web content. You submit targets, and you get investigation-ready intelligence. Une plateforme de Threat Infrastructure Intelligence que j'ai conçue et développée de bout en bout. Threat infrastructure tracking has become a public, community-driven effort. Ransomware leak sites are monitored by open trackers, and phishing infrastructure is reported every day through feeds such as OpenPhish, URLhaus and ThreatFox. HuntingBadGuys ingests these feeds automatically, and its public intel page shares scans of ransomware and phishing infrastructure that anyone can browse. Le tracking d'infrastructures malveillantes est devenu un effort public et communautaire. HuntingBadGuys ingère ces feeds automatiquement, et sa page d'intel publique partage des scans d'infrastructures ransomware et phishing que chacun peut consulter.

J'ai automatisé la collecte de renseignement sur Telegram, où une grande partie de l'écosystème cybercriminel opère au grand jour.
